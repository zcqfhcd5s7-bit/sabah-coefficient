6 · How points are earned (UEFA Annex D, 2026/27)

  • Qualifying wins do not count for the club coefficient — only for the country ranking. A club's season score comes from where it ends up.
  • Eliminated before any league phase → fixed points on the Conference League scale: Q1 1.0 · Q2 1.5 · Q3 2.0 · play-off 2.5. (This is how Sabah earned exactly 2.000 in each of the last three seasons.)
  • League phase onwards: 2 points per win, 1 per draw. Guaranteed minimums: UECL 2.5 · UEL 3.0.
  • Position bonus after the league phase: UCL 12.0 → 6.0 (everyone 25th–36th still gets 6.0!) · UEL 6.0 → 0 · UECL 4.0 → 0.
  • Knockout rounds reached (R16, QF, SF, Final): UCL +1.5 each · UEL +1.0 · UECL +0.5.
  • 5-year total can never be below 20% of Azerbaijan's country coefficient (currently ≈ 3.713 — not relevant, Sabah is above it).
